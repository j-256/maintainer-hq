# Your projects, in one place

Maintainer HQ is an online operator workspace for the projects you maintain. Bring together repositories, hooks, monitoring, secret operations, and accountable activity without making your laptop the control plane.

## Self-hosted availability

Maintainer HQ is self-hosted. The maintainer-operated deployment is private and does not offer public accounts. Use the [development](development.md), [secure hosting](hosting.md), and [offline release](release.md) guides to run an independent installation. ## Know which kind of information you are reading

**Expectations** describe what you want. **Evidence** records what a source observed and when. **Activity** records changes and attributed work reports. Saving an expectation, linking a monitor, or reporting a passing test does not independently prove that a service is healthy.

HQ distinguishes missing, incomplete, stale, and failed evidence. Its reviewed operations show exact targets and preserve uncertain outcomes so you can recover deliberately.

## Public guides, private operations

This site contains product documentation, not workspace data. Your deployment's dashboard requires sign-in and workspace membership. Documentation search runs against this static site; it does not search your repositories, Activity, credentials, or provider resources.

Use [the product model](concepts.md) to choose where work belongs, then follow the guide for the operation you need. Provider-specific limits and credential requirements remain explicit in each integration's contract.
